ABOUT US

Who We Are

Kusina Pilipinas is a family-run restaurant serving classic Filipino dishes made the way they are cooked at home. We opened with one simple idea: everyone deserves a warm plate of real Filipino food, whether they grew up with it or are trying it for the first time.

From savory adobo and sinigang to sweet halo-halo and leche flan, every recipe is cooked fresh daily using local ingredients and traditional methods passed down through generations.

What Makes Us Different

  • Home-style recipes cooked from scratch every day
  • Fresh, locally sourced ingredients
  • Regional Filipino favorites, from Luzon to Mindanao
  • A welcoming space for families, friends, and barkada

Our Mission

To share the rich flavors and culture of the Philippines through honest, delicious food, and to make every guest feel like family at our table.
